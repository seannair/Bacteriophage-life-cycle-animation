Lysogeny is patience. The prophage builds nothing and spends nothing: it is copied for free with the host chromosome and inherited by every descendant, silent for thousands of generations. Then the DNA is damaged, the repressor is cut in half, and the same genome that cost the cell nothing costs it everything.
